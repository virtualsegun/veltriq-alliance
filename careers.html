A note on recruitment fraud

Veltriq Alliance never asks candidates to pay a fee at any stage of recruitment. Every genuine vacancy is listed on this page, and we only contact candidates from an @veltriqalliance.com.ng email address. If you receive a job offer claiming to be from us that does not meet these points, please let us know at sales@veltriqalliance.com.ng.
