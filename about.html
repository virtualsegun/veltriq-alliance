About us

Technology partners, in the truest sense of the word

Veltriq Alliance is a Lagos-based IT systems integrator helping Nigerian organisations build, secure and run the infrastructure they depend on.

Who we are

Built on the idea of alliance

Our name says how we work. We don't drop off equipment and disappear. We take time to understand your operations, design solutions that fit, and stay alongside you to keep them running.

From a single office network to a multi-site datacenter, we bring together the skills you'd otherwise need several vendors for: systems integration, infrastructure, networking, security and ongoing support.

Our team is based in Ikeja, Lagos, so we can be on site quickly and we understand the realities of doing business here, from power stability to connectivity and compliance.

01Our mission

To give every organisation we serve reliable, secure and well-run technology, delivered by a partner who takes full responsibility for the result.

02Our vision

To be the most trusted IT infrastructure and security partner for businesses across Nigeria and West Africa.

Technology partners
